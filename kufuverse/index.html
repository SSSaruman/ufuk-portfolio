© Good to know(KFV® — 06)FAQ

A few
good questions.

01What type of projects do you take on?+

Creative direction, product and web design, brand systems, game UI, character and world development, and AI-led creative workflows.

02Can you work with an existing team?+

Yes. I can collaborate with studios, developers and production teams on a focused part of a project or across its visual direction.

03Do you use AI in your work?+

When it makes sense. AI is part of the research and production toolkit — design decisions, visual consistency and final quality still matter.

04How can we start a project?+

Send a short brief by email, including the challenge, desired outcome and timing. We'll take it from there.
